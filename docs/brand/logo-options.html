IdeaZen logo options

Six concepts, each built on one idea. Judged on distinctiveness, memorability and how they hold up at 16px (the favicon). Wordmark shown in Schibsted Grotesk Bold.

1

The highlighted line

Idea. A spec document with its key line highlighted. It is literally what the product produces, and it echoes the highlighter used across the site.

Why it sticks. The yellow bar bleeding past the text lines is the hook.

2

Route Z

Idea. The Z of IdeaZen drawn as a git branch path: commit nodes at each bend, ending in a yellow dot where the project ships.

Why it sticks. A letter that is also a commit graph. Developers read it instantly.

3

Open ring (the Zen circle)

Idea. An enso, the brush circle from Zen practice, left open. The yellow dot is the commit that closes it: an idea made real.

Why it sticks. One shape, one dot. Works at favicon size and carries the "Zen" in the name.

Idea. A highlighter pen mid-stroke, leaving the yellow bar behind it. Turns the site's highlight device into the brand mark.

Why it sticks. Nobody in dev tools has a pen. The tilted marker plus swipe is unmistakable.

Idea. A checkbox whose tick escapes the box. The step from "idea" to "done", and the progress tracking built into every plan.

Why it sticks. The tick breaking the frame gives it motion; a checkbox is universal to anyone who has used a to-do list.

6

iZ monogram

Idea. A lowercase i with a yellow spark for its dot, beside a bold Z. Reads as the name and gives the idea a literal spark.
